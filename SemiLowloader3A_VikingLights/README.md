# Semi Lowloader 3A — six three-way Viking markers

Full mod: [`FS25_semiLowloader3A.zip`](../FS25_semiLowloader3A.zip), version 1.0.0.3.

Each of the six fitted lamps has white forward guides, amber outward side guides and red rear guides, intensity 20. Both LODs are controlled by the towing vehicle's normal lights through native `defaultLight lightTypes="0"`. Attach the trailer and turn on the truck's regular lights.

The uploaded Viking model had no side guide. Twelve side guide shapes (six lamps, two LODs) reuse the original ring geometry with an outward rotation. The original shape binary, fixture positions, trailer physics, ramps, configurations and existing road/brake/reverse/indicator/beacon controls are preserved.

The upload omitted the Viking emission and dirt maps referenced in OneDrive. The mod includes red, white and amber emission DDS files; missing dirt masks use the native neutral vehicle mask. Original dirt detail may differ. The unrelated KKMaskin logo texture was also absent; its existing model, material and original reference remain unchanged.

Replace the previous ZIP with this complete file, keep the exact mod filename, and restart FS25. XML, mappings, source asset identity, all 36 native light controls, emission colors and ZIP CRC are validated. Decoded source geometry checks establish the white/red directions and placement of the new 24 mm side guides against the rubber housing. Blender previews illustrate geometry only. No FS25 runtime test was performed.
