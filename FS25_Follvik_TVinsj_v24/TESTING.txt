V24: front and rear hook winches each accept 100 tonnes total and deliver up to 980.665 kN (100 tonne-force).
Walk up to either winch, R to take hook; walk to a machine and aim at its collider within 4m; R attaches.
Hold Z to pull, X to pay out, Shift+R releases. Start the engine before pulling. Cable range 60m.
Equal reaction force remains on the 40-tonne chassis: traction, brakes and anchoring affect recovery.
Vehicle attachments use a direct hook instead of wrapping a log chain around the entire machine.
Vehicles must be loaded in physics; same vehicle/attached own equipment and static scenery are rejected.
Single-player mod; in-game physics and visuals not tested in this environment.
Disable previous T-Winch versions before loading.
