The download now includes all six previously missing artwork textures. **These are newly authored replacements; the original images were absent from the uploaded vehicle.** The two name decals have white italic lettering, a dark outline and a transparent background. The transport signs have black lettering on yellow panels. All six DDS files include alpha support and full mip chains.

The **Name layout** shop option retains two choices:

- J. Kristensen left / M. Kolbræk right (default).
- M. Kolbræk left / J. Kristensen right.

The supplied name meshes and positions are preserved. The grille sign uses **FS25_interactiveControl**: one click point opens/closes it; the other cycles **TUNG LAST → LANG LAST → BRED LAST** while open. The selected panel is saved through the native IC controller. The hidden advert panel also has a supplied SCANIA texture.

Replace `FS25_ScaniaS.zip` and restart FS25. Existing Briod high beams remain at 200 and white Briod DRLs at 40, with the 1000 m / 120° setup. Other fitted lights, IC code, vehicle settings and binary geometry are unchanged.

Checks passed for the six DDS files at all 12 mip levels, transparent name backgrounds, local model resources and archive CRC. An independent audit confirmed the intended changes. **Not tested in FS25/GIANTS Editor; the rendered UV fit and folding appearance still need an in-game check.**
